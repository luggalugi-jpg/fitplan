2. Principle: your data stays on your device

GainPilot works without a user account and without its own server. Everything you enter – meal plan, meals, weight, workouts, runs, supplements, goals and settings – is stored only locally in the app's storage on your device. We don't receive this data and can't see it. Exceptions are the optional leaderboard, the optional account with cloud backup and an optional Premium subscription (section 3).

The app contains no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and does not share data for advertising.

Information such as body weight, nutrition and training data may be health data. It is only processed on your device to provide the app's features.

3. Features that use the internet

Only the following features connect to third-party services, and only when you trigger them yourself:

Photo analysis (optional): Only when you have a photo analysed is it sent, together with an instruction and a random device ID, to the GainPilot server at Supabase (data centre in Paris, EU, see above) and forwarded from there to Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; processing in the USA is also possible, based on the EU standard contractual clauses). We use the paid service of the Google Gemini API; under its terms Google does not use the photos to train its models and only keeps them briefly to detect abuse. We do not store the photo. The server only counts how many analyses a device ID uses per day (daily limit); these counters are deleted after 7 days. If you store your own Google Gemini API key under “Photo analysis”, the photo goes directly from your device to Google instead, under the terms you agreed with Google; your key stays only on your device and is not included in backup files. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by starting the analysis. You can withdraw it at any time by no longer using the feature.

Barcode search: If you scan or enter a barcode, the product number is sent to Open Food Facts (Open Food Facts, 21 rue des Iles, 94100 Saint-Maur-des-Fossés, France) to retrieve nutritional values. For technical reasons your IP address is transmitted. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the feature you requested).

Web version: If you use the app in the browser, it is delivered via GitHub Pages (GitHub B.V., Netherlands / GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub processes technically necessary access data such as the IP address. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure provision of the website).

Leaderboard (optional): Only if you explicitly consent under “Leaderboard” does the app send data to our server: a random user ID (without email address or real name), your nickname and avatar, for each training day the number of completed exercises and whether the planned workout was complete, your training streak, your best set per exercise (weight, reps and the estimated max weight derived from them), your fastest run of 3 km or more in the last 60 days (date, distance and duration), the results of counted break games with start and end time, and the friends groups you create or join. If you set your gym under “Friends”, your current location is sent to the server once, only to find gyms nearby – it is not stored. What is stored is the gym's name and location (rounded to about 10 m), that you train there and when you last changed it. Optionally, sex, age group and body weight are added so that you're only compared with similar people. From this, strength and running scores, the monthly game season, the overall rating and the monthly league are calculated. Other participants see your nickname and avatar together with your score points (strength, running, games, overall) and rankings in the leaderboards, as well as your points in your league group. Members of your friends groups and everyone who has set the same gym also see your league tier and monthly points. Others don't see the gym's location, only its name and – when choosing on site – the approximate distance. Others don't see individual weights, running times or game results. Others don't see sex, age group and body weight. Unusual values and reports from other participants are stored to check for abuse. The server is operated by Supabase Pte. Ltd. (65 Chulia Street #38-02/03, OCBC Centre, Singapore 049513) as a processor; the data centre is in Paris (EU). Where Supabase accesses data from outside the EU, this is based on the EU standard contractual clauses. The legal basis is your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by ending your participation under “Data & backup” – all leaderboard data on the server is then deleted immediately.

Account and cloud backup (optional): If you create an account under “Account & cloud”, we store your email address and password (only as an encrypted hash) on our server to sign you in, send you confirmation and password links and protect your account. We use an email service provider as a processor to send these emails. If you switch on cloud backup, a compressed copy of all app data – including health data such as weight, nutrition and training, but without the photo analysis key – is transferred and stored. It is replaced with each new backup. Server, processor and standard contractual clauses are the same as for the leaderboard (Supabase, data centre in Paris). The legal basis for the account is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (feature you requested), for cloud backup your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can switch off and delete the cloud backup at any time or delete your account under “Account & cloud” – account, backup and leaderboard data on the server are then deleted immediately.

Premium subscription (in-app purchase, optional): If you take out a Premium subscription in the app from the Play Store or App Store, payment is handled exclusively by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) or Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland) under their own responsibility; we don't receive any payment or account data. To manage the subscription – checking whether Premium is active, renewal and restoring purchases – we use RevenueCat, Inc. (1032 E Brandon Blvd #3003, Brandon, FL 33511, USA) as a processor. This involves processing a random app user ID (if you take part in the leaderboard or have an account, your server ID), purchase and transaction data (product, purchase and expiry date, price, currency, store country), technical details such as operating system and app version, and the IP address. Processing takes place in the USA; the transfer is based on the EU standard contractual clauses. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the subscription contract) and, for statutory retention obligations, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. The app also stores locally on your device whether Premium is active. If you take part in the leaderboard, the server also stores whether Premium is active for you – this determines how many people your own friends groups can hold. There are no purchases and no transfer to RevenueCat in the web version.

4. Permissions

Camera: only for scanning barcodes and for photos for photo analysis, each only when you start the feature. Notifications: only for local reminders that you switch on yourself (end of rest, training, water, meals); they're created on the device, not via a server. Location: only so that the break games are unlocked at your gym – when saving a gym and when you start a game. The location is only compared with the saved gym coordinates on your device and is not sent to us or third parties. Only the coordinates of your gyms are stored, on the device and in backup files you create yourself.

5. Backups and diagnostic log

Backup files and the diagnostic log are only created if you create them yourself. You decide where to save or send them. The diagnostic log contains technical details for troubleshooting (e.g. app version, device type, error messages) and stays on your device until you share it.

6. Storage period and deletion

Your data stays stored until you delete it in the app under “Data & backup” or uninstall the app. Leaderboard data on the server is deleted as soon as you stop taking part; account and cloud backup as soon as you delete the account. Subscription data at RevenueCat is deleted once it is no longer needed for managing the subscription and legal retention obligations. If you deleted the app without ending your participation, email us your nickname and we'll delete it.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent given (Art. 15–21 GDPR). As we don't store your app data, you can exercise most rights directly in the app. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.

9. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The version published in the app applies.
