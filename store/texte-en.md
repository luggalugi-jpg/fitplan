## Description (Play max. 4000, Apple max. 4000)

```
GainPilot brings nutrition and training together in one plan – clear, works offline and no account required.

PLAN YOUR FOOD
• Over 3,000 fitness recipes with nutritional values, ingredients and step-by-step instructions
• Automatic weekly suggestion that fits your calorie and protein goal, also vegetarian or vegan
• Shopping list sorted by aisle, with items you already have to tick off
• Create your own dishes, favourites and “never suggest”

DAY BY DAY
• Calorie ring and macros (protein, carbs, fat) at a glance
• Tick off meals or log freely, scan barcodes (Open Food Facts)
• Water and supplements, reminders you can set up freely
• Optional: estimate meals from a photo (with your own Google Gemini key)

STRENGTH TRAINING
• 55 training plans from proven systems such as full body, push/pull/legs, PHUL, 5×5 and GVT
• Tick off sets and the rest timer starts by itself – with notification
• Smart progression: suggestions for more weight, hints for plateaus and deload weeks
• 3D animations with highlighted muscles for the most important exercises
• Share new personal bests as an image

RUNNING
• 55 running plans from beginner to marathon, matched to your race date
• Guides for every session: pace, heart rate zones, technique
• Log runs with distance, time, pace and heart rate, weekly kilometres and personal bests

LEADERBOARD (OPTIONAL)
• Strength and running compared fairly by weight and age class
• Overall rating with tiers from Rookie to Legend and a monthly league with promotion and relegation
• Friends groups via code or link
• Gym leaderboard: who's the strongest at your gym?
• Nickname only – no email, no real name

BREAK GAMES & LEVELS
• Seven short games for the rest between sets that never last longer than the rest itself
• XP, athlete levels and achievements for training, runs and personal bests

PROGRESS
• Weight history with 7-day trend, analysis with calorie recommendation and weekly review

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
No ads, no tracking. Your entries stay on your device. Only if you switch on the leaderboard or cloud backup yourself is the bare minimum sent to our server in the EU.

A simple guide to every feature is built right into the app. Available in English and German.

Note: GainPilot does not replace medical or nutritional advice. Nutritional values and plans are guidelines.
```
