Health notice

GainPilot is a tool for planning nutrition and training. The app is not a medical device and does not replace medical, nutritional or sports medicine advice.

Calorie needs, nutritional values, portion sizes, supplement amounts, training and running plans are guideline values and templates. They don't take into account illnesses, medication, pregnancy or individual capacity. The calculation of your needs is an estimate (Mifflin-St Jeor formula). Photo analyses and product data from Open Food Facts can be inaccurate.

Talk to a doctor before a significant change in diet, before taking supplements and before starting intensive training, especially if you have pre-existing conditions. Stop training if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
